How this is measured

The IBRL score is published by ibrl.wtf, a community tool from Jito that analyses leader behaviour block by block. It combines three independent components:

ComponentWeightWhat it rewards
Slot Time40%Building blocks quickly — a perfect score needs 550ms or less on a handoff slot, 400ms on a continuation slot. Past that the score decays exponentially.
Vote Packing15%Processing votes early — a perfect score needs 90% of vote transactions inside the first 48 PoH ticks. Including no votes at all scores 0.
Non-Vote Packing45%Spreading work across the block — half from using 50% of compute in the first 32 ticks, half from an even compute distribution across the 64 ticks (Gini coefficient).

IBRL = 0.40 × Slot Time + 0.15 × Vote Packing + 0.45 × Non-Vote Packing. Avg is the mean IBRL score over the last {{ history }} epochs, skipping epochs where the validator produced no blocks. Δ is the change from the previous epoch. Trend plots the same {{ history }} epochs, newest on the left; a gap means no blocks that epoch. Scores of 95 or above are shown in green, 90 to 95 plain, 80 to 90 in yellow, 70 to 80 in orange and below 70 in red.

Validator names, logos and stake come from the Solana Foundation API and are matched to IBRL on the identity pubkey.

A score built from a handful of blocks moves a lot epoch to epoch. The ≥ 32 blocks filter drops those rows when you want a steadier read.
